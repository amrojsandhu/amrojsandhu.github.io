Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't the beta free?

We'd love to offer LastPage for free, but the high-quality text-to-speech voices that make it so effective are powered by TTS APIs that carry real costs per character read. Even during beta, every sentence you listen to incurs an API charge on our end. Charging a small fee lets us keep the servers running, maintain voice quality, and continue building features—without cutting corners on the listening experience.

Can I use my own ebooks?

Yes. You can import your own ebooks and documents directly into LastPage. We support common formats so you can listen to anything in your personal library, not just content we provide.

Is there a mobile app?

A dedicated mobile app is on the roadmap. For now, LastPage works in your mobile browser and is optimised for phone screens, so you can listen on the go without needing an install.
