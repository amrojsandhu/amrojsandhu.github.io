Frequently Asked Questions

Is the beta free?

Yes — the beta is free for 30 days. Enter your email to subscribe, and you'll be redirected straight to the app after signup. This gives you a full month to try LastPage before we share the next steps.

Can I use my own ebooks?

Yes. You import your own EPUBs directly into LastPage. We don't provide a content library — you bring the books you already own. This keeps the app focused on the reading experience, not on selling you content.

Is there a mobile app?

LastPage is a PWA — a web app that installs directly on your phone from the browser, no App Store needed. On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen." On Android, tap the three-dot menu and "Add to Home Screen." It sits on your home screen and works like a native app.
