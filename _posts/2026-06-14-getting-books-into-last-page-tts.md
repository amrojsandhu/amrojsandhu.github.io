# Getting Books into Last Page TTS

Last Page TTS works with EPUB files — the most common ebook format outside of Kindle.

Here's everything you need to get started, including free books you can download right now.

---

## How to Upload a Book

1. Tap the **+** button on your library screen
2. Select the EPUB file from your device's downloads folder
3. The book appears in your library instantly
4. Tap it, hit play, and start listening

That's it. No account needed for the book. No DRM checks. If it's an EPUB file on your device, it works.

## Where to Find EPUBs

**For free classics:**

[Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) — the best-formatted free ebooks on the internet. Every book is carefully edited and designed. Start here.

[Project Gutenberg](https://gutenberg.org) — 60,000+ free books. Less polished than Standard Ebooks but vastly larger catalogue.

**For books you want to buy:**

[Kobo](https://kobo.com) — sells EPUBs you can download directly to your device. Same titles as Kindle, same prices, no lock-in. After purchase, download the EPUB from your Kobo account and upload it here.

[Smashwords](https://smashwords.com) — large catalogue of independent authors. Many books sold DRM-free as EPUBs.

[O'Reilly](https://oreilly.com) — if you read technical or business books, O'Reilly sells DRM-free EPUBs directly. Best source for professional nonfiction.

---

## Why Not Kindle Books?

Kindle uses a proprietary format that cannot be exported as EPUB. Books purchased on Kindle cannot be uploaded to Focus Reader or any other reading app.

If you buy books going forward, Kobo gives you the same titles with files you actually own and can use anywhere.

---

## Having trouble?

If your EPUB isn't loading, the file may be DRM-protected. DRM-protected files are locked to specific apps and cannot be opened elsewhere. The books listed above are all DRM-free and will work without issue.
